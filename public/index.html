How It Works
BCBA-First Design — Three Steps to a Submission-Ready PA

No training required. No templates to fill. Clinical data in — complete PA draft out.

1

Enter Patient Data

Select state, payer, and ABA CPT code (97153–97158). Fill in patient diagnosis, assessment scores, BCBA info, and family-training details.

2

AI Generates Payer-Specific Draft

AuraComply AI generates a complete 11-section PA draft in 60 seconds, embedded with Medicaid- or commercial-payer-specific wording and caps.

3

BCBA Review and Approval

BCBA reviews the draft, checks the compliance and denial-risk score, and approves before download. No draft can be exported without a clinician-review gate and signature placeholders.

Compliance

  • Compliance score 0–100, converted into a payer-specific denial-risk score for each ABA service line.
