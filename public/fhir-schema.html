AuraComply AI outputs structured PA-ready data that can be mapped directly into FHIR-style bundles. Clinics, EHRs, and revenue-cycle platforms using FHIR-based PA APIs can plug AuraComply AI drafts into standard prior-authorization workflows without manual re-entry.

What the Schema Covers

  • Patient, payer, and state metadata
  • Service codes (CPT 97153–97158), units, frequency, and period
  • Medical-necessity blocks (ICD-10, functional-status, SMART goals)
  • Required documents (assessment, BCBA report, family-training log)
  • Payer-specific rules (unit caps, required docs, modifiers)
  • Compliance and denial-risk scores with warnings
  • BCBA review gate — no export without clinician approval
CMS-0057 Prior Authorization Rule (2027): All major payers must support FHIR-based prior authorization APIs by 2027. AuraComply AI is designed to produce structured output that aligns with this mandate — protecting your clinic's workflow investment today and tomorrow.
